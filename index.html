My Story

My security foundation was built at IIT Kanpur and sharpened through research with SPRITZ Lab at the University of Padova — work that produced a 98% IDS detection accuracy on RPL-based IoT networks. That research curiosity translated directly into practice at AWS, where I spent two and a half years securing more than 15 high-risk services, including Amazon Bedrock's GenAI platform, remediating over 150 critical vulnerabilities in cloud-native environments.

Working deep inside financial AI infrastructure at AWS built an intuitive understanding of how financial systems are architected, attacked, and protected. Personal investing ran in parallel — studying capital allocation, public markets, and eventually unlisted equities. Bridging Gap gave that dual fluency a formal channel: building AI-powered research pipelines and deal evaluation frameworks for India's pre-IPO and private market ecosystem.

Private and unlisted markets are where technology can create the most transformative change — transparency, reachability, and democratized access that public-market innovation largely bypassed. Securing that infrastructure from the ground up, applying GenAI to compress research cycles, and understanding the business risk behind every technical decision: that intersection is the focus.
